## Which Mycopunk weapons are worth trying?

Looking for the best weapon in *Mycopunk*? Start with the **Gunship Cannon** if you want a flexible pick, or try the **Shocklance** and **Plate Launcher** if you’re willing to build around their strengths. Those are player recommendations, **not a verified ranking**: the mods you have and the way you like to fight can change which weapon works best for you.

The [official Mycopunk Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) confirms that weapons use grid-based upgrades and that missions grant new mods. It does not rank individual weapons. The comparisons below come from a [Steam Community discussion about Mycopunk’s best guns](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3247750/discussions/0/680734492841903390/?l=japanese), where players describe their own builds and preferences.

## The strongest player-recommended options

- **Gunship Cannon — a flexible starting point.** One player calls it their best general-purpose choice. Another favors its area-of-effect setup for ordinary encounters but suggests a more specialized single-target build for larger enemies. If you’re unsure where to invest your mods, this is the clearest all-around recommendation in the discussion.
- **Shocklance — a high-investment pick.** A player praises its combination of movement and damage, while cautioning that it needs substantial upgrades to reach its potential. Try it if you want to commit to a specialized build rather than judge it only in its base form.
- **Plate Launcher — strong, but less straightforward.** One player favors it for both individual targets and groups and reports that it helps supply ammo to their other weapon. They also say its projectile gravity takes practice. Another player finds its movement and screen effects uncomfortable, so its feel may be decisive.
- **Trident — a build-dependent alternative.** The discussion’s original poster prefers a setup involving water and lightning. That’s a useful example of a weapon succeeding through a particular mod combination, not evidence that it outperforms the options above.

These are individual reports from a discussion dated November 2025. They are useful starting points, but they do not establish a current consensus or account for every possible build.

## How to choose a weapon for your build

**Pick a role before chasing a tier list.** If you want a broadly useful weapon, begin with the Gunship Cannon. If you enjoy investing in a specialized setup, look at the Shocklance. If you don’t mind learning an unusual firing pattern, give the Plate Launcher a try.

Then check the mods you actually own. The official description says you can mix, match, and stack upgrades wherever they fit in a weapon’s grid. Players in the discussion likewise stress that upgrades can substantially change how a gun performs. A weapon you can build effectively now may serve you better than a highly recommended one whose key upgrades you lack.

Finally, pay attention to handling. The thread includes both praise for the Plate Launcher and a report that its effects make it unpleasant to use. No damage claim can tell you whether a weapon feels comfortable in your hands.

## What this comparison cannot verify

The official store description does not provide weapon damage figures, drop rates, unlock requirements, or a best-in-slot list. The linked player discussion contains personal experiences, not controlled comparisons. For that reason, this guide does not assign tiers or promise that a named mod will produce the same result in every build.

## FAQ

### What are the Mycopunk best weapons for a new build?

Based on the cited player discussion, the Gunship Cannon is the most straightforward general-purpose recommendation. The Shocklance and Plate Launcher are also praised, but players describe more investment or a steeper learning curve with those choices.

### Is the Shocklance better than the Gunship Cannon?

There is no verified head-to-head answer. Players praise the Shocklance’s potential with upgrades and the Gunship Cannon’s versatility. Your available mods and preferred playstyle are better reasons to choose between them than an unsupported universal ranking.

### Does the weapon matter more than its mods?

Both matter, but mods can change the comparison dramatically. The official Steam description confirms that weapon upgrades fit into customizable grids, and players in the cited discussion warn against judging guns solely by their unmodified forms.
