## The short answer

Missing the Carver, Globbler, or Accelerator? A [Mycopunk Steam community discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3247750/discussions/0/800091928768258361/) describes a different route for each: collect parts for the Carver, find a transponder to start the Globbler’s unlock mission, and complete Oxythane Breach for the Accelerator. **These are player reports, not officially confirmed unlock instructions.** The discussion does not establish exact part locations or any level requirement.

## How players report unlocking the Carver

A player in the Steam discussion says the Carver’s first part is in Titan City Underground. They report finding three more parts in other biomes, mentioning “Singularity” Gravity Farms and excluding Ice Floes. According to that account, arranging all four pieces into a diamond completes the unlock.

The report does not identify precise spots for the parts. Treat those areas as leads rather than a room-by-room route.

## How players report unlocking the Globbler

The same player describes a sequence in “Singularity” Gravity Farms:

1. Press three buttons to open a room reportedly near the center of the map.
2. Find the transponder inside that room.
3. Activate the transponder in the hub.
4. Complete the mission it unlocks to receive the Globbler.

No button positions are provided in the discussion, so this is a quest outline rather than a complete location guide.

## How players report unlocking the Accelerator

Two participants in the Steam discussion say completing the Oxythane Breach mission unlocks the Oxythane gun, which the detailed reply calls the Accelerator. That reply refers to the *special* Oxythane Breach mission but explicitly says the player does not know whether a minimum threat level or player level is required to access it or earn the weapon.

## Weapon unlocks versus upgrades

Finding a weapon and upgrading it are separate questions. The [official Mycopunk Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) confirms that missions grant mods for a grid-based upgrade system. It does **not** confirm the Carver, Globbler, or Accelerator routes described above, nor does it list their drop rates or unlock requirements.

## FAQ
