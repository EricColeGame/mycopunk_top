## The short answer

Trying to choose an elemental build in Mycopunk? The available evidence does not establish a reliable, complete list of what each element does. The official game description confirms a grid-based mod system, but it does not explain individual element effects, damage values, or status interactions.

## Elements mentioned by community sources

A search excerpt from the [Mycopunk Wiki’s Elements page](https://mycopunk.fandom.com/wiki/Elements) lists Fire, Shock, Acid, Decay, Rot, and Wet. The full page was unavailable for review, so that list should not be treated as a verified guide to how the effects work.

A separate excerpt from a [Steam Community discussion about status effects](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3247750/discussions/0/596288191849455422/) says Fire, Shock, and Corrosion apply damage over time. It does not provide enough context to verify their differences or how they interact with other mods. Notably, it says *Corrosion* where the wiki excerpt says *Acid*; the excerpts do not establish whether those names refer to the same effect.

## What the official description confirms

The [official Mycopunk Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) says missions award mods that players can fit, mix, and stack in upgrade grids for their robots and weapons. That makes mod combinations central to building a loadout. It does **not** confirm which elemental effects exist, their application rates, their damage, or whether one element is stronger than another.

For now, treat precise elemental build advice and numerical claims cautiously unless they come with a source that documents the relevant in-game behavior.
