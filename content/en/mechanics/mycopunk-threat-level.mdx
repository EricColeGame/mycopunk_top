## What Is Mycopunk Threat Level?

An indexed excerpt from the [Mycopunk Wiki’s mission page](https://mycopunk.fandom.com/wiki/Mission), collected October 21, 2026, says players can select a threat level for each mission. A [Mycopunk preview from TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/mycopunk-preview/) describes the game becoming significantly harder as threat level rises, even with a few upgrades.

Those are limited excerpts, not fully available pages or a verified breakdown of the current game. They support the basic difficulty distinction, but not a precise list of what changes between levels.

## Is Threat Level the Same as Swarm Intensity?

The mission-page search excerpt lists threat level **and** a choice of three swarm intensities for each mission. That suggests they are separate selections. It does not explain what each swarm intensity changes or how the two settings interact, so don’t assume one is simply another name for the other.

## How Should You Choose a Threat Level?

If a higher setting feels too difficult, try a lower threat level before your next mission. That advice follows the preview’s observation about increasing difficulty; it is not a claim that a particular level is best for every player or build.
