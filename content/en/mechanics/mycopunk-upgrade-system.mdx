## How does the upgrade system work?

Your loadout in *Mycopunk* depends on the mods you can fit into its upgrade grids. That matters because upgrading is about combining mods for your robots and weapons, not just choosing a single preset path. According to the [official Mycopunk Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/), you can mix and stack mods, with the grid’s available space determining what fits.

## How do you get more mods?

Each mission grants new mods for your upgrade grids. As you complete missions, you gain more pieces to combine and can revisit how you’ve built your robot and weapons. The official description does not specify a particular mod reward list or a method for obtaining a specific mod.

## What kinds of builds are possible?

The official examples show how varied the system is intended to be: you can build toward a healing-support role, equip a chainsaw with a deflector shield, or turn your robot into a bomb. You can also upgrade futuristic weapons and power tools.

Those are examples of possible directions, not a verified ranking of the strongest builds. If you’re deciding what to try, start with the role or weapon you want to use, then see which of your available mods fit its grid.

## What details are still unconfirmed?

The official description establishes the grid-based system, mission-granted mods, and the ability to mix and stack what fits. It does **not** explain exact grid layouts, mod shapes, placement controls, or whether mods can be rotated. It also does not confirm hidden upgrade unlocks or a separate grid-solving tool.

## FAQ

### What is the Mycopunk upgrade system?

It’s a grid-based system for modifying your robots and weapons. You fit mods into upgrade grids and can mix or stack the ones that fit.

### Do missions give you upgrade mods?

Yes. The official game description says every mission grants new mods.

### Can you make a support build?

Yes. Healing support is one of the build directions the official description explicitly mentions.
