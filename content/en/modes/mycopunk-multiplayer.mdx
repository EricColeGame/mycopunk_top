## Does Mycopunk have multiplayer?

Yes. **Mycopunk supports four-player online co-op**, and you can also play solo. That matters if you’re deciding whether to pick it up with friends: the [official Mycopunk Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) confirms both options, though it does not explain every detail of how online groups are formed.

## What do you do together?

In co-op, you take on planet-side missions as members of the New Atlas Hazard Crew, a squad sent to confront a fungal catastrophe. The game centers on fighting enemies with futuristic weapons and power tools while customizing your equipment through grid-based upgrades.

The official description also points to different ways to shape a build, including a healing-support approach. Between missions, the crew can spend time together aboard an orbital dropship. These are confirmed parts of the game, but the listing does not specify how mission progress or upgrades are shared between players.

## Can you play alone instead?

Yes. Steam lists single-player alongside online co-op, so a group is not required to play Mycopunk. The available official details do not establish whether solo play changes mission objectives, enemy behavior, or rewards.
