## Is Mycopunk cross platform?

Planning to play Mycopunk with friends on different systems? Platform support matters before anyone buys a copy. As of October 29, 2026, the official Steam listing confirms Windows support and online co-op, but it does **not** confirm a console edition or crossplay.

That means there is no verified way to say that players on different platforms can join the same game. Online co-op and cross-platform play are different features; confirmation of one does not establish the other.

## Which systems are supported?

The [official Mycopunk Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) lists Windows as supported. It does not list Mac or Linux support. The available official source also does not verify PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo editions.

If your group uses different systems, check the official listing for each system before making plans to play together. A platform release would still need separate crossplay confirmation.

## What multiplayer is confirmed?

Steam lists single-player, multiplayer, and online co-op for Mycopunk, including **four-player co-op**. Friends can team up for the game’s missions, but the listing does not specify crossplay between different systems.

For a group playing on Windows, the confirmed online co-op listing is the useful detail. For a mixed-platform group, crossplay remains unconfirmed.
