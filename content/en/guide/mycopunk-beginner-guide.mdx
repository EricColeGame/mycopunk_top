Mycopunk can throw a lot at a new crew: mobile enemies, planet-side objectives, and an upgrade grid full of possibilities. The simplest starting approach is to keep moving, complete your mission objectives, and use each run’s mods to improve a setup you understand. The [official Mycopunk Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) confirms the game’s core systems; some tactical tips below come from a 2025 hands-on article and may differ from the current release.

## Learn Your Movement Before Chasing Damage

Start by finding a comfortable way to move while aiming. Mycopunk’s enemies attack in hordes, and the 2025 [Prima Games beginner tips](https://primagames.com/tips/best-beginner-tips-mycopunk) recommend staying mobile rather than firing from one spot. Its writer highlights sprinting, sliding, and character-specific movement abilities as ways to create space.

Try your chosen character’s abilities early in a mission so you know how they help you reposition. If the action feels visually overwhelming, Prima also recommends checking the settings for UI distortion, bloom, screen shake, and motion-sickness options. Check the settings available in your version rather than relying on older menu values.

## Watch What Enemies Leave Behind

The official description notes an unusual combat risk: when you break enemies apart, other enemies can pick up what they drop. Pay attention to the fight after a takedown instead of assuming that enemy is no longer relevant.

Prima’s writer also advises using the terrain to make room when pressured. Look for a path that lets you keep moving and return to the objective, especially when playing solo. That is a practical approach, not a guarantee that every arena offers the same escape options.

## Keep Your First Upgrades Simple

Mycopunk gives you mods after missions and lets you fit them into upgrade grids for your robot and weapons. You can mix and stack mods that fit, so you do not need to commit to a single build idea immediately.

For your first few missions:

- Check which mods you earned before changing your setup.
- Fit upgrades around a weapon or role you already enjoy.
- Make a few changes at a time so you can tell what helps.

The official listing describes possibilities ranging from healing support to unusual weapon combinations, but it does not establish a best beginner build or a ranked upgrade path. Experiment instead of treating any one layout as mandatory.

## Put the Mission Ahead of the Horde

Mycopunk supports solo play and four-player online co-op. Whether you play alone or with friends, keep track of what your current mission asks you to do. Prima’s 2025 guide advises completing objectives before lingering to fight extra enemies and suggests leaving once the job is done if the crew is struggling.

That same article reports that missions become harder over time, but it does not establish a precise scaling rule. Treat its advice as a reason to avoid unnecessary delays—not as a verified timer or a promise about rewards. In co-op, make sure teammates are ready before moving on from an area or ending a mission.

## FAQ

### What should I focus on first in this Mycopunk beginner guide?

Practice moving while fighting, follow the mission objective, and review the mods you receive afterward. Those habits give you a foundation without requiring a particular character or build.

### Can I play Mycopunk alone?

Yes. The official Steam listing confirms single-player as well as four-player online co-op.

### Is there a best beginner weapon or upgrade build?

The available official information does not verify one. Mycopunk’s grid system encourages mixing mods that fit, so start with a setup you can use comfortably and adjust it after each mission.

### Do I need to defeat every enemy before finishing a mission?

Not necessarily. Prima’s 2025 guide advises prioritizing objectives over clearing every enemy, while noting that some missions may call for enemy takedowns. Follow the requirements shown for the mission you are playing.
