## The short answer

Mycopunk is worth a closer look if you want a mission-based looter shooter built around experimental weapon upgrades and online co-op. That distinction matters: the grid-based build system appears central to the game, so its appeal depends on whether you enjoy tinkering between fights as much as fighting. The [official Mycopunk Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) confirms single-player and four-player online co-op, but the available sources do not establish how well either mode plays in practice.

Developed by Pigeons at Play and published by Devolver Digital, Mycopunk released on October 20, 2026, after an Early Access period.

## What you’ll do in Mycopunk

You play as part of the New Atlas Hazard Crew, a group of robots sent on planet-side missions amid a fungal catastrophe. The store describes combat against hordes of enemies that can use corrupted technology. It also says enemies can pick up parts dropped by other enemies—a distinctive detail to watch for if you’re interested in how fights develop.

Between missions, you return to an orbital dropship. The official description presents it as a place to spend time with friends and explore more of the game’s setting, but it does not establish how much there is to do there.

## Is the upgrade system your kind of thing?

Mycopunk’s clearest selling point is its grid-based customization. Missions award mods that you can fit into upgrade grids for your robots and weapons. The store emphasizes combining and stacking upgrades, with possibilities such as a healing-support setup or a shield-equipped chainsaw.

That makes the buying question fairly simple: **do you want to experiment with builds?** If arranging mods and trying unusual combinations sounds fun, Mycopunk’s stated design fits. If you’d rather use a fixed loadout without spending time on upgrades, consider watching gameplay before purchasing. The sources do not verify particular build rankings, drop rates, or how long it takes to unlock options.

## Check your platform, PC, and price

Steam lists Mycopunk for **Windows**, not macOS or Linux. Its listed minimum calls for Windows 10 64-bit, 8 GB RAM, a GTX 960, Radeon R9 380, or Arc A380, and 10 GB of storage. The store associates that minimum with low settings at 1080p and 30 FPS. Its recommended graphics cards are an RTX 2080, RX 5700 XT, or Arc A750, associated with high settings at 1080p and 60 FPS. These are store-listed targets, not independently tested performance results.

The [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3247750/) showed a regular US price of **$14.99** and a **$7.49** promotion when checked on October 29, 2026. Prices and offers can change, so check the current total and your region before buying. Steam also lists a third-party EULA requirement.
